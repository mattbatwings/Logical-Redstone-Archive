Unpacks boxes of discs, reads their contents with a jukebox, and repackages the discs.
## Input
Noteblock on top to start reading,
Boxes containing discs in chest on top
## Output
Signal strength pulses at the front
Repackaged boxes in chest on bottom
## Speed
8gt between pulses (hopperspeed)
## Considerations
- Minimum 2 items per box.
- Any non-disc item can be used as 0, but stackable non-disc items might not be repackaged in the right order.
## Notes
Dimensions: 2 wide tileable, 8 high stackable, 10 blocks deep.
